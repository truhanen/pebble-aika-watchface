A readable watchface.

Extremely bold analog hands, with the minute hand extending past the edges of the screen, for the highest visibility.

Widget values are drawn on top of the clock hands. Values are what counts.

Widgets can show their measurement units (°C, %, ...), instead of ambiguous & duplicated icons.

Widget units & icons are drawn under the clock hands. It's alright to see them most of the time. The hands are the more important component.

The clock center (optionally) "breathes" up & down following the minute hand's direction. This reduces the distortion from the minute hand's changing length, and makes the face feel closer to a square than the rectangular screen really is.

Widget fonts, sizes, positions, icons, & units are fully customizable. Available widget categories include:
- Time
- Date
- Weather
- Rain
- Health
- Sleep
- Battery
- Crypto
- Currency
- Electricity spot price
- Tuya sensors & switch states
- Bluetooth

Other configurable features:
- Colors
- Night mode
- Battery & Bluetooth warning signals (border & widget)
- Weather units & data sources (Open-Meteo or FMI)
- Rotating widget groups
- Language options
- Periodic vibrations

The comprehensive widget system is adopted from another open source watchface, Sykerö TimeStyle, github.com/Sykero-Software/TimeStylePebble, which is based on TimeStyle by freakified.

The analog hands are adopted from an earlier watchface of mine, Nyquist, github.com/truhanen/pebble-nyquist-watchface.
