Nyquist is a bold analog watchface with an edge tick ring, three configurable widget columns (left, right and centre) and optional rotating widget groups. Each widget has its own font and can show an icon, a unit label or nothing. Widgets cover weather (temperature, forecast low/high, UV index, maximum rain in the next 1–12 hours from FMI or Open-Meteo), health (steps, distance, sleep, heart rate), battery level and estimated battery days, electricity spot price, crypto and currency rates, Tuya smart-home values and LEDs, and an alternate time zone. Night mode, colour themes and a battery/Bluetooth warning border are included. Configure everything from your phone.
